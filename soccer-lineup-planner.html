Roster and positions each girl can play

Tap a position to turn it on or off for that player. Auto-fill only puts a girl in positions that are turned on. Turn on the first chip (goalkeeper) for your two goalies. A player with nothing turned on is left out of auto-fill, which is handy when someone misses a game. Use the playing time setting to have auto-fill give a girl more or less time than the rest.
