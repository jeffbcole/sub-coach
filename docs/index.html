Sub Coach

Soccer lineups, substitutions & game timer for youth coaches · iPhone and iPad

Plan fair rotations in seconds, get an alert at every substitution with exactly who goes in and out, and keep your assistant coaches in sync over Bluetooth. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Features

  • Auto-fill builds a rotation with even playing time and longer stretches on the bench, not constant in-and-out.
  • Respects the positions each player can play, a goalkeeper per half, and who's at today's game.
  • Game Day clock with substitution alerts, a lock screen countdown and the Dynamic Island.
  • 4 to 11 players on the field, with formations for each size.
  • Share your roster, or broadcast a live game, to coaches standing nearby.
  • Print a one-page lineup for the sideline.
